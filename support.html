Support

Tuning

How do I get a steady reading?

  • Play one string at a time and let it ring.
  • Find a quiet spot. A TV, a fan or another instrument can confuse the tuner.
  • Hold the phone near the sound hole, pickup or body.
  • Tune up to the note from slightly below. Strings settle better that way.

The note reads sharp right after I pluck

A string vibrates hard right after the pluck and its pitch runs a little high for a moment. Wait a second or two for the needle to settle, then read it.

The tuner doesn't hear anything

Pitchline needs the microphone. If you tapped "Don't Allow", open iPhone Settings, then Pitchline, and turn on Microphone. A case or a hand over the bottom of the phone can also block it. Audio is analyzed on your iPhone and never recorded or sent.

Where are the settings?

Tap the round arrow at the top right to turn the amp around. The back panel has the A4 trim, note names, the instrument Pitchline opens with, haptics, the covering color, Restore Purchases and the Signal check.

Why does it show H instead of B?

German note names call B-flat "B" and B "H". On the back panel, pick C D E, DO RE MI or H.

Metronome

Does the metronome keep playing in the background?

Yes, with the screen locked or in another app. After a phone call it carries on by itself. The Lock Screen shows the tempo with Stop and plus/minus keys.

Ear trainer and chords

How do I choose which intervals to practice?

On the EAR channel, flip the keys switch from ANSWER to CHOOSE and tap intervals to add or remove them. At least two stay in the drill.

Can I hear a chord?

Tap the diagram or STRUM. The arrows step through other shapes of the same chord.

Practice log

How is practice time counted?

Only while the PRACTICE switch is on. Time is logged to the channel you're on. Sessions shorter than 30 seconds aren't logged, and one stretch counts for three hours at most, so a phone left on the music stand overnight doesn't fill the log.

Pro

What does Pro add?

More instruments, alternate and custom tunings, strobe mode, the full metronome, every interval and note in the ear trainer, all chord types and four amp coverings. It's a single purchase, not a subscription.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

On the back panel tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with. The hang tag has a Restore link too.

Can my family use Pro?

Yes, Pro supports Family Sharing.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. You can ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
