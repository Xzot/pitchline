Readings are estimates

The tuner and metronome work from the phone's microphone and clock. Pitch readings can be affected by noise, the microphone, the instrument and how you play, and can be wrong. They are meant to help you tune and practice. Check by ear if something sounds off. Tune carefully, since strings can break when they are tightened too far.
