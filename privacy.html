Privacy Policy

Pitchline collects nothing. It has no servers, accounts, analytics or ads, and it includes no third-party code that collects data. In the App Store privacy label it is listed as Data Not Collected.

Effective date: October 29, 2026. Pitchline is made by Vlad Shchuka ("we").

Microphone

The tuner listens through the microphone only while the TUNE channel is on screen, and the Signal check only while it is open. The sound is analyzed in memory on your iPhone to find its pitch. It is never recorded, stored or sent, and we never receive it. The microphone turns off when you leave the tuner or the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings (instrument, tuning, A4, note names, metronome, covering, custom tunings), your ear trainer scores and your practice log (when sessions started and ended and how long you spent on each channel) are saved on the device only. We don't receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Lock Screen and widgets

While the metronome plays, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show the tempo and practice time. The widget and Control Center button only start and stop the metronome. None of this leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

Pitchline Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; the app only asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns Pro.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: shchukavlad4@gmail.com.
