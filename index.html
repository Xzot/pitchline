A practice amp for your phone

Pitchline puts a tuner, a metronome, an ear trainer and a chord chart on one panel, laid out like the front of a small practice amp. Press a channel key and play. The tuner stays free for guitar, bass and ukulele, with no ads and no account.

Channel 1 · Tune

A backlit meter with a real needle

Play a string and the meter shows the note and how many cents you are off. Three lamps say flat, in tune or sharp. Pitchline picks out the string you play, or you can tap a string, set the switch to Hold and sound its reference tone to tune by ear.

  • Guitar, 4-string bass and ukulele in standard tuning, free
  • Chromatic mode for any note
  • A4 trim from 415 to 466 Hz, note names as C D E, Do Re Mi or German H
Channel 2 · Tempo

A metronome you turn like a knob

Turn the tempo knob or tap the footswitch, 20 to 300 BPM. One lamp lights per beat. The click keeps going with the screen locked, and the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show the tempo with Stop and plus/minus keys.

Channel 3 · Ear

Learn intervals by ear

Pitchline plucks two notes and you name the gap: a fifth or a major third. Or it plays an A and then another note, and you find it on the keys. It keeps your streak and shows which interval needs more work.

Channel 4 · Chords

Chord diagrams for guitar and ukulele

Pick a root and a chord type and the chart draws the fingering, with open shapes first and barre shapes further up the neck. Tap the diagram to hear it strummed.

Practice log

An hour meter for practice

Flip PRACTICE on when you start. The counter rolls, the time is split by channel, and the log shows the last seven days and your streak. It all stays on your iPhone.
