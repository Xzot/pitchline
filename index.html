A tuner that stays free, and a metronome in the same app

Pitchline listens through the microphone and shows the note and how many cents you are off. Pluck a string and watch the needle. Turn the peg until it turns green. The tuner has no ads and no subscription, and it never asks you to pay before you can tune.

  • Chromatic tuner with a needle, note name and cents
  • Guitar, 4-string bass and ukulele in standard tuning
  • Reference tones, so you can tune by ear
  • A4 calibration from 415 to 466 Hz
  • Metronome from 20 to 300 BPM with tap tempo, which keeps playing when the screen is locked
  • Works offline. No account. Nothing you play is recorded or sent anywhere.

Free and Pro
